All of the subjects that I know

When it comes to topics, the usual ones I think of are Music, Arts, Physical Education, Health, Geography, and many other subjects; But here are all the subjects I've ever heard of:

Index Generic Description
⒈ English This might be one of the most common subjects in the world, which teaches the English language, and how to use it for things like literary works.
⒉ Filipino Also very common (natively), but teaches the Filipino language.
⒊ Music Teaches various types of music, explores native and foreign culture, let's us sing songs, and even teaches something about ourselves.
⒋ Arts This one let's us learn arts, explore native and foreign culture, as well as prominent place and people, and lets us expresses our creativity.
⒌ Physical Education Focuses on developing physical fitness, skills, teamwork, and sportsmanship.
⒍ Health Teaches the importance of our physical, mental, and social well-being, along with nutrition and safety.
⒎ Araling Panlipunan Teaches various events in Philippine history, geography, economics, civics and culture.
⒏ ESP / Values Education This subject lets use learn and study personal responsibilities, relationships, services, and faith. It helps us recognize, understand, and manage our emotions in healthy ways.
⒐ Science This subject allows us to explore the physical and natural world around us. Some examples are atoms, elements, electrons, and the nucleus.
⒑ Mathematics Focuses on numbers, mathematics specifically mastering linear equations, functions, shapes and polygons, and a lot more. Some examples are the Pythagorean Theorem, algebra, and trigonometry.
⒒ TLE Goes through many branches, including, IA/HE, AFA, and ICT. While these may not be for some curriculums, these are the main ones I've heard of.

Conclusion

In conclusion, these 11 subjects represent the entire landscape of what I've learned and heard about in education. From language subjects like English and Filipino, to complex ones like Math and TLE, I think each and every one of them play a role in making everybody more educated and connected to native and foreign culture.
