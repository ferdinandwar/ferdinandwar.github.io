Do you happen to know Jonathan is a traditional Hebrew name meaning "God has given" or "gift of God?"

It derives from the Hebrew name Yehonatan (יְהוֹנָתָן) or its contracted form Yonatan (יוֹנָתָן).

The name first appears in the Old Testament (Hebrew Bible). Jonathan was the oldest son of King Saul and a loyal, devoted best friend to David. Their bond is a symbol of friendship and sacrifice.

While common in Jewish tradition for centuries, the name grew widely popular in English-speaking countries following the Protestant Reformation and surged further during the 20th century. 

Additionally, A Hasmonean bronze prutah (a tiny ancient coin) minted over 2000 years ago during the reign of the Jewish king Alexander Jannaeus (103 to 76 BCE).

Archaeologists and volunteers have unearthed these tiny bronze pieces in places like the Temple Mount Sifting Project in Jerusalem. Because they were minted in ancient, archaic script rather than the everyday script of the era, scholars believe they were designed to intentionally remind people of their deep historical roots
