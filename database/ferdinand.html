Hey, it's me, it's Ferdinand!

Have you ever thought of your name recently?

Do you happen to know, 

That your name literally means "ready for the journey" or "bold traveler"?

Derived from ancient Gothic roots (fardi meaning journey, and nanþs meaning brave), the name Ferdinand carries a legendary history of adventure.

While it belongs to famous history makers like Ferdinand Magellan (the explorer whose crew achieved the first global circumnavigation), it also famously belongs to Ferdinand the Bull, the giant, peace-loving literary and movie character who defied all expectations by preferring to sit quietly and smell flowers instead of fighting!
